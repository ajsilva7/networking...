### IPv4 Range

0 – 255

8 bits

00000000 = 0

11111111 = 255

Total values = 256 (0–255)

| Class | First Octet | Purpose                 |
| ----- | ----------- | ----------------------- |
| A     | 1–126       | Large Networks          |
| B     | 128–191     | Medium Networks         |
| C     | 192–223     | Small Networks          |
| D     | 224–239     | Multicast               |
| E     | 240–255     | Research & Experimental |
